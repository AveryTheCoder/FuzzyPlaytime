# Fuzzy Playtime - Game Mechanics

## TIME SYSTEM
- 1 real second = 1 game minute
- A full in-game day = ~24 real minutes
- The game lasts as long as it needs to
- Time affects monster behavior and hostility

## PLAYER
- First-person 3D exploration
- Health: 100 HP
- Movement: WASD, sprint with Shift
- Inventory system (collect items, notes, keys)
- Crosshair for aiming/interacting

## COMPUTER DESKTOP
- XP-style desktop environment behind the front desk
- Password-locked login (password found on bloody notecard, randomly generated per playthrough)
- Passwords stored in game_data.json
- Browseable file system:
  - CCTV/ [PASSWORD LOCKED]
    - 02-23-2011-main-entrance.mp4 (normal)
    - 02-24-2011-main-entrance.mp4 (normal)
    - 02-25-2011-main-entrance.mp4 (something off)
    - 02-26-2011-main-entrance.mp4 (Hour of Joy)
  - Documents/
    - daily-customer-report-02-23-2011.docx (normal)
    - daily-customer-report-02-24-2011.docx (abnormally high)
    - daily-customer-report-02-25-2011.docx (abnormally low)
  - Passwords/
    - PinManager.exe
    - PasswordManager.exe

## MONSTERS

### Fuzzy (Mascot)
- Design: Huggy Wuggy with western hat, green
- Starts friendly, turned evil by other monsters temporarily
- Kills same way as Huggy Wuggy (chase and stuff into body)

### Teacher Melody
- Design: Mommy Long Legs with western gear, maroon
- Walks you around exhibits, quizzes on nature/history
- Angered by wrong answers or making her wait too long
- QUIZ MECHANIC: Dialogue and interactive, depending on quiz type
- Kills by removing glove and stabbing face with prototype claws

### Mr. JJ
- Design: Western cowboy
- Lurks in American Revolution exhibit at midnight
- Extremely hostile, will try to kill any way possible

### John and Jeff (One Monster)
- Design: Two static western guys in pickup truck display
- Only attack if jingle button pressed 15-17 times repeatedly
- Kills by extending neck via spring and biting head off

### Miss Indiana
- Design: 1700s Indian
- Always nice, answers exhibit questions
- Dies at the end saving the player (ally NPC)

### Miss Hawk
- Design: Hawk with cowboy hat
- 11:00 AM - 4:00 PM: Patrols exhibit, answers questions (friendly)
- 4:30 PM - 5:00 PM: Stalks from vents
- 6:00 PM - 7:15 PM: Vent death trap - drag in and rip face off
- Stay away from vents during danger hours

### Old Guy Patriot
- Design: Continental soldier
- Armed with musket
- Shoots if you don't follow American Revolution exhibit rules
- Kills via musket shot or bayonet

### Potty the Parrot
- Design: Green and yellow parrot, scarred
- Yaps constantly, fights with Miss Hawk
- Violent at random times
- Kills by pecking your skull

## STORY BEATS
1. Wake up disoriented at NCFEACL
2. Enter building, see Fuzzy statue with spyglass
3. Find computer behind counter
4. Log in with password from bloody notecard
5. Browse files and folders
6. Explore and meet 4 monsters (still, not hostile yet):
   - Fuzzy (friendly)
   - John and Jeff
   - Miss Hawk
   - Teacher Melody
7. Learn about characters through exploration
8. Navigate monster schedules and hazards
9. Miss Indiana dies saving you
10. Escape the center

## GAMEPLAY LOOP
- Explore exhibits during safe hours
- Use computer to uncover lore
- Avoid hostile monsters based on time
- Solve puzzles and answer Melody's quizzes
- Collect items and information
- Progress through story beats
